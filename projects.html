THE PROGRESSION

What changed from
Day 01 to Day 06.

The useful part is not the project count. It is whether the decisions become more disciplined from one build to the next.

DAY 01 / GULF MCR

Brand atmosphere

Focused on translating a Middle Eastern cafe identity into a responsive visual experience instead of using a generic restaurant layout.

DAY 02 / TASTEOLOGY

Editorial interaction

Pushed typography, food storytelling and carousel behavior while learning where visual ambition starts hurting mobile usability.

DAY 03 / ARTISTA PERFETTO

Research discipline

Used real business media, reduced decorative excess and strengthened information order, keyboard behavior and mobile QA.

DAY 04 / MOOR COFFEE

Mobile composition

Kept the cafe personality while treating the phone layout as its own composition rather than a compressed desktop page.

DAY 05 / SOMETHING MORE PRODUCTIVE

Community hierarchy

Organized events, social energy, local character and practical actions into a clearer content structure.

DAY 06 / ALWAYS THE SAME

Context and clarity

Made the daytime specialty-coffee identity, venue context, offering and visit information understandable without losing personality.
