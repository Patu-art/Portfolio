About my journey ↗

THE MEMORY LINE / DAYS 01—06

Not six cards. Six checkpoints in the same journey.

I started the challenge to force myself to stop waiting for the “perfect project.” Each day leaves a small memory: a design decision, a mistake, a better mobile rule, or a clearer way to research before building.

The point is progression, not pretending every build is the final answer.

DAY 01 / THE FIRST SHIP

Gulf MCR

The first public challenge build. It made the idea real: research a business, build around its identity, deploy the result and put it in front of someone instead of keeping it inside VS Code.

Lesson / atmosphere still needs structure

DAY 02 / PUSH THE VISUAL LANGUAGE

Tasteology

The second build pushed typography and food storytelling harder. It was a useful reminder that a memorable visual idea only works when navigation, hierarchy and mobile behavior remain understandable.

Lesson / typography can carry identity

DAY 03 / RESEARCH BEFORE DECORATION

Artista Perfetto

This is where the SITEPRO thinking became more obvious: use real business information, reduce invented decoration, order the content properly, and test the experience instead of judging only the screenshot.

Lesson / research beats decoration

DAY 04 / COMPOSE FOR THE PHONE

Moor Coffee

A compact cafe build where the mobile experience mattered as much as the desktop composition. The aim was to keep personality without letting playful details become visual noise.

Lesson / mobile is a composition, not a shrink
Case study ↗Live ↗

DAY 05 / DESIGN AROUND COMMUNITY

Something More Productive

The site had to organize social energy, events and local character into something visitors could actually use. That pushed the work beyond styling and further into content hierarchy.

Lesson / community content needs hierarchy

DAY 06 / MAKE THE CONTEXT CLEAR

Always The Same

A daytime specialty-coffee concept operating from an existing venue meant the site had to communicate context clearly: what the brand is, what people can get, where it lives and why the experience feels distinct.
