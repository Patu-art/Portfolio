WORKFLOW

The challenge is not “make a pretty site.” It has a loop.

A repeatable loop makes the challenge useful as training, portfolio evidence and business outreach at the same time.

01 / RESEARCH

Understand before designing.

Study prospects, compare them, choose the stronger opportunity, inspect brand, location, content, current online presence and customer needs.

02 / BUILD

Use SITEPRO thinking, not a template.

Choose a direction based on that business. Build responsive HTML, CSS and JavaScript and verify the real browser experience.

03 / OUTREACH

Send proof.

Deploy the demo, contact the business with a short relevant pitch and learn from the response—or lack of one.

WHY THIS MATTERS

The valuable thing should be the difference between Day 01 and Day 100.

Six builds are enough to start seeing the process change. The next ninety-four are where that system gets stress-tested.
